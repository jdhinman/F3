Hello all, I'm a brand new user, clearly. I've always wanted there to be an option to keep the Desert Star and White Balverine Statue as trophies instead of being forced to cash them in one of two ways. I don't suppose that already exists, so how hard would something like that be?

As for the final empty slot, the supposed missing Shadelight Book would be preferred, but I'm sure there are other trinkets in the game a clever modder might find more appropriate or interesting to fill that gap. I'm just not crazy about the idea of still having the Balverine head if I later managed to get the coveted statue, but, still, I'd be quite happy just having the two empty slots filled as well.
