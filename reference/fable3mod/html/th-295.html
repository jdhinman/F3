This is really odd. Stuff like that generally only happens with a modded version. Vanilla Fable 3 is actually pretty robust.
Can't leave the dressing room not even with the Esc key?

What you could try is this (I see no alternative):
Make a backup of your save games (C:\Users\Username\Saved Games\Lionhead Studios\Fable 3) and copy them elsewhere.
Delete your profile's save folder (probably a bunch of numbers or it might actually be a name). This is to prevent cross-contamination.
Play up to that point to see if it happens again.

Sometimes save files just bork for no reason whatsoever.
