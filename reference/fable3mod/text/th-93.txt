 Keshire wrote on Tue, 07 June 2011 14:34 This should basically give you everything in the game. (Apart from npc weapons and DLC). You'll need to find the names for npc weapons and use the Inventory.AddItemOfType function. 
 
From the weaponinventory.lua Debug.GiveAllMeleeWeapons()
Debug.GiveAllRangedWeapons()
Debug.GiveAllSpellGauntlets()
Debug.AddAllInventoryItemsF2()
Debug.AddAllInventoryItems()
