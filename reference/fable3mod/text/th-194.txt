 they should be in 
art\gui\gui_textures.bnk.dat 
or 
globals\globals_textures.bnk.dat 
first you need to extract .bnk files from streaming.bnk 
then place .bnk in .dat files folder 
then you can open it with BlackDemon's BNK Utils 
after you find posters, probably you need to convert them to DDS format with this tool
