Actually I wrote this "content.xbx" by myself and for those who want to add mods, there is only two main values that need to change, is NAMEs (maybe is not needed) and ContentID (definitely NEED) 
so, how to get ContentID for you own mod? 
you can just write random numbers, but easiest way is calculate SHA-1 hash of "yourMod.bnk.dat" 
also remember to specify it in "package_collection_info.xmb" too, with prefix 0x
