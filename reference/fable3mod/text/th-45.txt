001C62E8 offset

#08A1AB45 
-> CarryingComponent #A3B2D002

 DO 00061EE4 D 52226653 DummyName | Character.Carry.SheathWeaponFront

 DO 00061EE4 D DA0B5122 DummyName | Character.Carry.SheathRangedWeaponBack
