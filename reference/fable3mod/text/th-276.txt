For the hunter lodge no modding is required. You just have to put the dlc into the DLC folder specified by whichever gfwl emulator you are using. Somewhere in between dweller and brightwall quests you'll receive the hunter's lodge deed automatically as a gift (along with a bunch of other dlc items). Or anytime after those quests.
